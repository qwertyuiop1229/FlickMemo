# Modern icon concept

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Preview concept; current production icon has not been replaced.

Create ONE exceptionally refined contemporary app icon for FlickMemo, a fast lightweight notes utility. Art direction: the restraint and precision of current Apple app icons, the simple sculptural geometry of Microsoft Fluent icons, and the recognizability of a Google product mark, with an ORIGINAL identity and no resemblance to any existing company's logo. Keep only three colors: warm ivory, deep charcoal, and warm burnt orange. A single original abstract folded ribbon symbol, made from just TWO broad softly rounded interlocking strokes, one charcoal and one burnt orange, suggesting a quick thought being captured. Strong balanced silhouette, substantial negative space, exceptionally simple; no literal stationery, no document/paper outline, no notebook, no lines of writing, NO arrows or arrowheads, no paper plane, no pencil, no lettering. Allow very subtle premium soft-volume shading on the two strokes, clean satin surfaces, no shiny plastic, no glass, no harsh gradient, no texture. Center the compact symbol on a solid warm ivory rounded-square tile with very generous smooth continuous corners. The tile fills the square image, with genuinely transparent pixels outside its rounded corners. Crisp beautifully antialiased geometry, immediately legible at tiny sizes. Production-ready icon, not a presentation or mockup. No text, no labels, no multiple options, no extra elements. Square 1024x1024.
